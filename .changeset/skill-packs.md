---
'skilless': minor
---

Add skill packs. `skilless add <pack>` adds every skill a pack lists — from skilless.dev, any URL serving a pack, or a pack file — and `skilless update` brings in skills the packs you follow gain, and asks about ones they drop. `skilless add` also takes a skill's address, `skilless.dev/skills/<id>`, and `github.com/owner/repo/path` now reads as a path in the repo. `skilless packs list|create|delete` manages your packs on skilless.dev. A pack can include other packs; a pack that leads back into itself is refused.
